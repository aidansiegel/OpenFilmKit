# Overnight dogfooding, November 24, 2026

No scanner was attached, so the desktop app ran against a stand-in for the Plustek OpticFilm 8200i SE
that copies the real scan logs (its warnings, its progress steps, its open failure) and returns real
roll-35 negatives as its scans. Everything ran through the app's back end the way the window drives it,
plus a stretch in the real Electron window.

The run was paused at 10:17 pm at the Manager session's request: swap had reached 8.3 of 9 GB and the
internal disk was down to 806 MB free, the state that filled the disk on Monday. The long scanning soak
planned for the rest of the night never started. Everything below ran before that.

All fixes are on the `dogfood-2026-11-24` branch, not pushed or merged.

## Fixed

1. **A scan kept running after the app quit or crashed** (`0c14a6f`). Killing the back end mid-scan left
   the scan process and scanimage running for 16 to 36 seconds; they finished and saved the frame with
   nothing left to show it. Quit does the same, since Electron kills the back end and Python goes without
   stopping its child. On a real scanner, a restarted app could then start a second scan on a busy
   scanner. The back end now gives each scan a pipe it never writes to, and the scan stops, as Stop would,
   when that pipe closes. Checked in the real window: quitting mid-scan stops the scan 1.6 seconds later,
   recorded as stopped, and the next scan works.

   The first version of this fix made every successful scan look failed: the watcher thread held
   `sys.stdin`'s lock at shutdown and Python aborted (exit -6). Caught by the next dogfood scan, fixed in
   the same commit by reading the file descriptor directly.

2. **A save didn't match its preview** (`c42c0c2`). All 66 real negatives went through preview and a
   full-size save. Six saves differed from what the preview showed: up to 2% narrower and up to 24 levels
   off. Convert found the picture box and the black and white levels again, on a copy of a different size
   and on the full-size scan, where grain moves the extremes. Save now hands convert the preview's box and
   levels (the recipe records the crop as automatic and the levels as "as previewed"). Seven of the eight
   frames rechecked now differ by 2 to 4 levels, about what JPEG and scaling give. One overexposed frame
   (roll35-frame26) is still 9.6 off. Export, which builds no preview, still lets convert decide.

3. **Hidden `._` files on exported USB sticks** (`7816207`). On an exFAT stick, every exported picture
   had a hidden `._` twin: macOS's AppleDouble file for the `com.apple.provenance` stamp it puts on every
   new file. A TV, a photo frame or a Windows PC shows each one as a broken picture. The stamp can't be
   removed, but its `._` file can, and stays gone after a remount. Export now takes away each copy's twin,
   on macOS only, and only a file with AppleDouble's signature.

4. **Accessibility** (`5bd6edc`). axe-core 4.14.0 with WCAG 2.2 A/AA rules on Roll, Adjust, Scan, Queue
   and the Export sheet, in light and dark. Fixed: the frame cards' visible text didn't match their spoken
   labels (the name and status word ran together), the film card's label left out the words it shows, and
   the Scan screen's frame strip scrolled but couldn't take keyboard focus. No contrast failures in either
   theme. These are in `gui/src`; worth a look from the UI side.

## Checked and fine

- **Real negatives:** 66 scans, preview about 5 seconds, full-size save about 13 seconds. The back end
  peaks at about 2.25 GB during a save of a 575 MB scan and comes back down to about 700 MB between frames,
  so no leak. The source folder was read only, checked byte for byte before and after.
- **Empty frames:** 11 of the 66 are called empty. On the contact sheet all 11 are blank holder slots.
- **The app window:** 41 scans in 18 minutes, 17 of them with faults (jams, open failures once and twice,
  a busy scanner). Every one showed the right words, with no page errors. Memory stayed between 215 and
  677 MB with no upward trend.
- **Killing the back end mid-save:** nothing half-written was left, and a new back end opened the roll and
  scanned.
- **Export to a USB stick:** a first export, exporting again (all skipped, no duplicate copies), two
  changed frames ("(2)" copies), a full stick ("There isn't room..."), the stick pulled out partway (the
  remaining frames fail in words and the back end carries on), and Eject. A roll on a drive too small for
  another scan says so before scanning.

## Still open

- **Automatic turning gets about 10 of 55 pictures wrong**: upside down or sideways, mostly roll 27 and
  two in roll 48. Roll 27 also has a strong yellow-green cast and a scanner color warning on most frames.
  This is the weakness the November 21 bake-off found; too big to fix overnight.
- **The scanning soak didn't run** (paused for memory). Its harness is ready.
- **axe still flags some frame cards on the Roll screen** as label/content mismatches without saying
  which text, and the result changes between runs. Worth checking in the VoiceOver pass.
- **The `stop_when_parent_goes` regression test** checks a clean exit, but the abort it guards against
  only showed in a real scan, not in the small test process.
